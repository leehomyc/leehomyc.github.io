Why this course

Discover current research across arts and machine creativity, learn how different researchers frame real-world problems, and build a wider intellectual community. Students are expected to attend every week during the term in which they enroll.

What you will practice

Listen broadly.
Connect deeply.

01

Research awareness

Follow current trends, methods, and applications across neighboring fields.

02

Presentation craft

Observe how strong speakers explain problems, evidence, decisions, and limitations.

03

Research connection

Relate each seminar to your own work—or to a methodology outside your usual focus.
