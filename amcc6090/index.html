Course requirements

Questions,
answered.

AMCC 6090 is graded Pass / Fail, with participation accounting for 100% of the assessment.

Do I need to attend every seminar to pass?+

Plan to attend every scheduled seminar during the term in which you enroll. The approved course document says students are required to attend every week, and participation is the full assessment.

The document does not set a numerical absence threshold or state that one absence automatically results in a Fail. If an unavoidable situation prevents you from attending, contact the instructor promptly to discuss it.

How is the course assessed?+

The course is graded Pass / Fail. The listed assessment is 100% participation; no separate exam or assignment is listed. Attendance is recorded for each seminar, while the reflection and feedback fields are optional.

How many terms must I pass?+

MPhil students in Arts and Machine Creativity must register for and pass AMCC 6090 once before graduation. PhD students must register for and pass it in two terms before graduation.

How do I record attendance?+

Open the attendance form during the seminar date, choose the session, enter your name, student ID, and the course code shared in class, then confirm that you attended in person. The form opens at 6:30 PM and closes at 11:59 PM Hong Kong time.
