# AMCC 5160 · Quiz 07

Two technique questions, two art questions, and one reflection on a reading of your choice. 4 points each; 20 points total. Answer in your own words (1–3 sentences). Submit online at https://hyang.org/amcc5160/quiz-07/ before the end of the semester.

1. Technique: What is “drift” in long AI video, and what did Helios do about it? (4 points)

2. Technique: Wan-Dancer plans at two levels. What does each level do, and why does that help a minute-long dance? (4 points)

3. Art: Name four things an artist should keep so that an AI artwork can survive the tool that made it. (4 points)

4. Art: Who could claim to be the choreographer of a Wan-Dancer video? Name two candidates and give one reason for each. (4 points)

5. Reading reflection: Choose one of the three readings. State its main idea in one sentence, then say whether you agree or disagree, and why. (4 points)
