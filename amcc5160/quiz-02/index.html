011 point

A glass pear becomes ceramic. Which edit contract is most coherent?

  • A. Preserve its amber transmitted glow exactly.
  • B. Preserve its silhouette and blue stem; allow reflections to adapt.
  • C. Permit a new silhouette because the material changed.
  • D. Judge success only by how attractive the result looks.
021 point

A 1024 x 1024 RGB image becomes a 64 x 64 x 64 latent. What follows from counting scalar values?

  • A. There are 16 times fewer values and generation is 16 times faster.
  • B. The latent has 64 times more total values.
  • C. There are 12 times fewer values; this alone does not determine speed.
  • D. The stored file must be exactly 12 times smaller.
031 point

For v_guided = v_base + s(v_cond - v_base), what happens when s = 1?

  • A. The expression equals v_cond.
  • B. The condition is ignored and the expression equals v_base.
  • C. The prediction extrapolates beyond v_cond.
  • D. All source pixels are copied exactly.
041 point

Which statement best distinguishes an image reference from LoRA?

  • A. Both necessarily retrain the full model.
  • B. A reference guarantees identity; LoRA guarantees style.
  • C. They are two names for the same inference setting.
  • D. A reference conditions an output; LoRA changes learned weights.
051 point

Why can four attractive frames be insufficient evidence of video-edit quality?

  • A. Real videos should contain identical frames.
  • B. Errors may occur between samples or during occlusion.
  • C. Every change in camera position indicates identity drift.
  • D. Keyframe approval makes temporal evaluation unnecessary.
061 point

After approving an edited keyframe in the Runway reading’s workflow, which claim still needs testing?

  • A. Whether the approved frame displays the intended material and color.
  • B. Whether the approved still-frame composition suits the brief.
  • C. Whether the edit persists coherently through motion and reappearance.
  • D. Whether the approved keyframe retains the chosen subject silhouette.
074 points

One Euler update uses z = 0.20, step size = 0.10, and velocity = 0.60. Show the calculation, give the next z, and explain what one update does not prove about the final edit.

084 points

An illustrative tokenization reduces N from 4096 to 1024. Calculate the token reduction factor and the dense self-attention pair-score reduction factor. Explain why this does not establish an equal end-to-end speedup.

094 points

Your AFTER RAIN collage uses flat paper layers, but the reflection is sharply photographic. Give a critique tied to the visual language, propose one revision, and explain how you would compare versions.

104 points

A five-second ceramic-pear edit passes behind a column. State two invariants, identify a high-risk event, and describe evidence you would inspect before accepting the clip.

114 points

Using An Alien Mind, explain goal alignment and value alignment in your own words. Apply the distinction to a creative task and name one piece of evidence you would seek before trusting delegation.

124 points

Using Dan Koe’s essay, propose a small experiment to clarify a creative direction. Explain its connection to the reading, state an observable outcome, and identify one limitation of treating the essay’s promise as a general rule.
