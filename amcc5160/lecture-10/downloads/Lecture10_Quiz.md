# AMCC 5160 · Quiz 10

Two technique questions, two art questions, and one reflection on a reading of your choice. 4 points each; 20 points total. Answer in your own words (1–3 sentences). Submit online at https://hyang.org/amcc5160/quiz-10/ before the end of the semester.

1. Technique: A model that only replays its training images can get an excellent FID. Why? What does that tell you about what FID cannot measure? (4 points)

2. Technique: One signed image is re-saved and its Content Credentials disappear. Another is edited with its manifest kept, and it reads as invalid. What does each result tell you, and why does a missing credential not prove an image is fake? (4 points)

3. Art: Choose one work made with AI (an image, a video or a film). Critique it in four steps, one sentence each: describe, analyze, interpret, then judge. (4 points)

4. Art: You direct the Madison museum. Do you show Diffuse Control? Give one reason, and say which question you are answering: is it AI, is it good, is it fair, or is it allowed? (4 points)

5. Reading reflection: Choose one of the three readings. State its main idea in one sentence, then name one claim you would want to test and how you would test it. (4 points)
