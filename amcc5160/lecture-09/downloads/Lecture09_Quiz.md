Two technique questions, two art questions, and one reflection on a reading of your choice. 4 points each; 20 points total. Answer in your own words (1–3 sentences). Submit online at https://hyang.org/amcc5160/quiz-09/ before the end of the semester.

2. Technique: Why do 3D Gaussian splats render so much faster than a NeRF? Answer in one or two sentences. (4 points)

3. Art: Choose a place you would like to scan as a Gaussian splat. What would the copy keep, what would it lose, and whose permission would you need? (4 points)
