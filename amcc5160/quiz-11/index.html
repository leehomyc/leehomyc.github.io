014 points

Technique: Drift in long video (Lecture 07), a world model that forgets the red door (Lecture 09), and a robot whose small errors compound (Lecture 09) share one cause. Name it, and describe one technique from the course that reduces it.

024 points

Technique: Your main character's face changes from shot to shot in your A2 or A3. Choose two methods from Lectures 02, 05 or 08 (for example a reference image or IP-Adapter, LoRA, DreamBooth, or a motion latent like VASA-1). Say what each one supplies or trains, and give one trade-off between them.

034 points

Art: Choose one of the four debates (Lecture 07, 08, 09 or 10). Give your vote that night, your vote now, and one piece of evidence from a different lecture that moved or confirmed it.

044 points

Art: Write the credit block for the project you will present on November 17 or 24: models and versions, your human work counted, references, consent, and one plain sentence of disclosure.

054 points

Reflection: Which single idea from this semester most changed how you make or look at work? Name the lecture, and give one concrete example from your own project.
