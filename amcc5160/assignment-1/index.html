From Images to Film

A Generative AI Workflow

Create a 30–60-second audiovisual work using generative AI. Your work should communicate a clear idea, story, mood, or message. Possible formats include a short narrative, fictional advertisement, animated artwork, or experimental film.

Due September 30, 2026 · 23:59 HKT

Concept → Images → Video → Audio → Final edit

Your workflow

  1. Concept and storyboard

    Describe your idea and intended audience. Plan a sequence of 4–6 shots, considering what the viewer should see, hear, and feel.

  2. Generate and refine images

    Create the key images for your shots. Develop a consistent visual style and use editing or references to improve continuity across characters, objects, and settings.

  3. Animate the images

    Use image-to-video generation to turn your images into moving shots. Make deliberate choices about subject movement, camera movement, and pacing.

  4. Create the audio

    Use generative AI for at least one audio element: music, sound effects, ambience, or narration. Combine sound and images purposefully; dialogue is optional.

  5. Edit the final work

    Assemble your shots and audio into a coherent piece. Refine timing, transitions, sound levels, and titles or captions where appropriate.

What to submit

  • Final video: 30–60 seconds, with audio, in MP4 format.
  • Process report: 6–10 slides showing your concept, storyboard, key images, workflow diagram, tools, and selected prompts.
  • Iteration evidence: Two before-and-after examples explaining a problem, what you changed, and the result.
  • Reflection: 200–300 words on your creative contribution, the limitations you encountered, and what you learned.
  • Credits: Identify AI-generated content, manual edits, and external assets.
