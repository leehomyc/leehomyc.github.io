# AMCC 5160 · Quiz 11 (Review)

Two technique questions, two art questions, and one reflection. Each asks you to connect ideas from different lectures. 4 points each; 20 points total. Answer in your own words (1–3 sentences). Submit online at https://hyang.org/amcc5160/quiz-11/ before the end of the semester.

1. Technique: Drift in long video (Lecture 07), a world model that forgets the red door (Lecture 09), and a robot whose small errors compound (Lecture 09) share one cause. Name it, and describe one technique from the course that reduces it. (4 points)

2. Technique: Your main character's face changes from shot to shot in your A2 or A3. Choose two methods from Lectures 02, 05 or 08 (for example a reference image or IP-Adapter, LoRA, DreamBooth, or a motion latent like VASA-1). Say what each one supplies or trains, and give one trade-off between them. (4 points)

3. Art: Choose one of the four debates (Lecture 07, 08, 09 or 10). Give your vote that night, your vote now, and one piece of evidence from a different lecture that moved or confirmed it. (4 points)

4. Art: Write the credit block for the project you will present on November 17 or 24: models and versions, your human work counted, references, consent, and one plain sentence of disclosure. (4 points)

5. Reflection: Which single idea from this semester most changed how you make or look at work? Name the lecture, and give one concrete example from your own project. (4 points)
